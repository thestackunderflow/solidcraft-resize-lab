Use the solidcraft MCP tools to model a mounting plate in millimetres: 80 mm wide along X, 50 mm deep along Y and 5 mm thick (Z from 0 to 5), with one corner at the origin. Drill four 5 mm holes at the corners, each centred 8 mm in from both edges, and one 12 mm hole in the middle of the plate. Export it as STL to /lab/out/plate-80.stl. Make the width a parameter named width and build the plate so the holes follow it: the corner holes stay 8 mm from the edges and the 12 mm hole stays in the middle. Then change only the width parameter: to 120 mm, export /lab/out/plate-120.stl; then to 60 mm, export /lab/out/plate-60.stl. Report the bounding box after each export and any step that failed.
